A writ for the action
that cannot be undone.

When an agent leaves the chat and writes into a system of record, someone has to authorize it. Writ is that instrument: principal, decision, receipt.

Three objects

01

Principal

Human sponsor, agent, task, and purpose — bound together. Not a service account with a prettier name.

02

Decision

Allow, step-up, or deny at the moment an Action or tool is about to fire. Short-lived. Audience-bound. Revocable.

03

Receipt

An immutable record on the ontology: who, what, on which object, under which purpose, with which policy version.

Why Palantir AIP

Inside Foundry, agents are already principals with markings. The hole is the last mile: user token versus service identity versus on-behalf-of, and the hop out of Foundry into a clinical app, a checkout API, or an IdP. We build the receipt as ontology objects and hook it at Action time. Then we take it across the garden over MCP.

Cisco bought the inventory. Cyera bought the data graph. Palantir has not bought this layer. We are building it on the platform, not next to it.
